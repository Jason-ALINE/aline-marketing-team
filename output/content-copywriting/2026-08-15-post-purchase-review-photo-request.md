# Post-Purchase Review & Photo Request — Core Copy

Two versions below: one for webstore/direct buyers (email), one for Amazon buyers (policy-safe, short). Both are self-contained single-email asks — no assumed prior message in a sequence.

---

## Version 1: Webstore / Direct Buyer

**Subject line options:**
- Quick favor — how's the alignment tool working out?
- A minute of your time (and maybe a photo)?

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

You picked up an ALINE [A-3000 / A-3000W] a little while back — hope it's earning its keep on the shop floor.

We're a small manufacturer, and most of what people know about our tools comes from word of mouth on the floor, not marketing. That's the way we like it, but it means real feedback matters more to us than most companies. If you've had a chance to run an alignment job or two with it, two quick asks:

**1. Leave a review.**
A few honest sentences on [Google / our website] — what you used it for, whether it held up, anything you'd tell another millwright before they bought one. Good or bad, we want it accurate.

[Leave a review →]

**2. Send us a photo.**
If you've got a shot of the tool actually set up on a shaft — brackets mounted, dial indicator reading, whatever the job looked like — send it our way. Not a product-box photo, the real thing on a real job. With your permission, we may use it (and a line or two of what you told us) as a case study on our site, and we'd credit you and your company if you're good with that. No obligation either way — even without a testimonial, seeing the tool in the field helps us build stuff people actually want.

Just reply to this email with a photo attached, or send it to [email address].

Appreciate it — and if anything about the tool isn't working the way you expected, tell us that too. We'd rather hear it from you than have you quietly switch to something else.

[Name]
ALINE Manufacturing

## Version 2: Amazon Buyer (policy-safe)

**Use case:** Amazon's own "Request a Review" button is the safest default for this — it sends Amazon's neutral, pre-approved template and requires no custom copy from us. Use the version below only if sending manually (e.g., buyer-seller messaging) and keep it as close to this as possible. No incentive, discount, or exchange language of any kind.

**Subject line (if sending manually via Amazon messaging):**
- Your recent ALINE order

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Thanks for your recent purchase of the ALINE [A-3000 / A-3000W] shaft alignment tool set. We hope it's working well for you.

If you have a few minutes, we'd appreciate your honest review on Amazon — it helps other buyers evaluating alignment tools make an informed decision.

[Leave a review link — if permitted by current Amazon buyer-messaging policy]

If you run into any issue with the product, please reach out to us directly at [support email] first — we're happy to help.

Thanks again for your order.

ALINE Manufacturing

---

**Note on Version 2 photo ask:** Deliberately left out of the Amazon-side copy. Amazon's review policies restrict what a seller can request or imply is being collected outside the review itself, and photo solicitation tied to a review ask is a gray area worth avoiding rather than testing. If ALINE wants in-use photos from Amazon buyers, that should be a **separate, clearly non-review-linked ask** (e.g., a follow-up "how's it working, mind sending a photo for our site" message with no mention of reviews in the same message) — flagging this as a decision point rather than deciding it myself.

**Decision (Jason, 2026-08-15):** Amazon buyers will be handled via Seller Central's native "Request a Review" button instead of this custom copy. Version 2 above is kept for reference only — no sequence needed for Amazon.

## Key assumptions made
- Assumed buyer is addressed by first name and a real order/product reference (A-3000 or A-3000W) will be merge-filled by whoever sends this — no names invented here.
- Assumed ALINE has (or will have) a live Google Business review link and/or an on-site review/testimonial submission path — confirmed by Jason (2026-08-15): a live Google "request a review" link already exists.
- No customer quotes, stats, or case studies were fabricated — this is ask copy, not proof copy. Once someone replies with a review or photo, that becomes real source material for future case-study content.
- Kept the Amazon version deliberately generic and short per general Amazon review-solicitation policy norms — not a formal compliance review. Moot now given the native-button decision above.
- Tone/persona targets millwright, maintenance supervisor, or contract-firm PM as the reader — not a reliability engineer — consistent with company-brief.md.
