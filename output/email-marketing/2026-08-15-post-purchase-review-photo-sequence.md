# Post-Purchase Review & Photo Sequence — Webstore Buyers (A-3000 / A-3000W)

**Audience:** Recent direct/webstore buyers of ALINE's precision shaft alignment tool sets. Low-volume, high-touch, technical buyer (millwright / maintenance supervisor / contract-firm PM) — not a mass drip list.

**Status: DRAFT ONLY.** No email platform is connected for this team yet, and even once one is, these are built to be sent manually, one at a time, by Jason (or whoever owns the buyer relationship) — not queued as an automated trigger. Sending judgment (see "send conditions" per email) should stay a human call, especially email 2 and 3, since a buyer who already replied with an issue in email 1 shouldn't get the ask in email 2 as if nothing was said.

**Source copy:** `output/content-copywriting/2026-08-15-post-purchase-review-photo-request.md`, "Version 1: Webstore / Direct Buyer" section only. Amazon (Version 2) intentionally excluded — Jason's confirmed decision is to use Amazon Seller Central's native "Request a Review" button instead.

**Goal of the sequence overall:** Get a Google review and/or an in-use photo (potential credited case study) from recent buyers — currently the single biggest trust-gap lever for ALINE (no visible testimonials on site).

**Personalization tokens used:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{product_name}}` (A-3000 or A-3000W, whichever they bought), `{{sender_name}}`. No real data implied — fill before sending.

**Placeholders to fill before sending:** `[Google review link]` (live link exists, not yet supplied to this draft) and `[support email]`.

## Email 1 — Check-in (no ask)

**Position:** 1 of 3
**Timing:** ~2–3 weeks after delivery — enough time to have run at least one alignment job, not so long the purchase feels stale.
**Goal:** Open a low-pressure line of contact and gauge sentiment before making any ask. Doubles as an early-warning channel for product issues.
**Primary CTA:** Reply (only if something's wrong) — not a review/photo ask yet.
**Send condition:** Send to all recent buyers not already personally followed up with.

**Subject line variants:**
- How's the {{product_name}} working out?
- Quick check-in on your ALINE order
- Everything holding up on your end?

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

Wanted to check in — it's been a few weeks since your ALINE {{product_name}} order. Hope it's earning its keep on the shop floor.

No ask here, just curious how it's going. If anything about the tool isn't working the way you expected, reply and let us know directly — we'd rather hear it from you than have you quietly switch to something else.

{{sender_name}}
ALINE Manufacturing

## Email 2 — Review & photo ask

**Position:** 2 of 3
**Timing:** ~7–10 days after Email 1.
**Goal:** Get a Google review and/or an in-use photo (potential case study).
**Primary CTA:** Leave a review (photo request is the secondary ask, kept in the same email per the source copy's structure).
**Send condition:** Only send to buyers who did NOT flag a product problem in reply to Email 1. If a buyer reported an issue, that gets handled directly first — don't run this ask on autopilot into an open complaint.

**Subject line variants:**
- Quick favor — how's the alignment tool working out?
- A minute of your time (and maybe a photo)?
- Two quick favors, if you've got 5 minutes

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

Following up from our last note — hope the ALINE {{product_name}} is still holding up well.

We're a small manufacturer, and most of what people know about our tools comes from word of mouth on the floor, not marketing. That's the way we like it, but it means real feedback matters more to us than most companies. If you've had a chance to run an alignment job or two with it, two quick asks:

**1. Leave a review.**
A few honest sentences on Google — what you used it for, whether it held up, anything you'd tell another millwright before they bought one. Good or bad, we want it accurate.

[Google review link]

**2. Send us a photo.**
If you've got a shot of the tool actually set up on a shaft — brackets mounted, dial indicator reading, whatever the job looked like — send it our way. Not a product-box photo, the real thing on a real job. With your permission, we may use it (and a line or two of what you told us) as a case study on our site, and we'd credit you and your company if you're good with that. No obligation either way — even without a testimonial, seeing the tool in the field helps us build stuff people actually want.

Just reply to this email with a photo attached, or send it to [support email].

Appreciate it — and if anything about the tool isn't working the way you expected, tell us that too. We'd rather hear it from you than have you quietly switch to something else.

{{sender_name}}
ALINE Manufacturing

## Email 3 — Soft follow-up (only if no response)

**Position:** 3 of 3
**Timing:** ~10–14 days after Email 2.
**Goal:** One light nudge, no guilt, then stop — respects that this is a busy technical buyer, not a lead to chase.
**Primary CTA:** Same as Email 2 (review link), restated briefly.
**Send condition:** Only if no reply, review, or photo was received after Email 2. Do not send a third time after this regardless of outcome.

**Subject line variants:**
- Still happy to hear how the alignment tool's working
- No pressure — just following up
- One more nudge (feel free to ignore)

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

No worries if you haven't had a chance — just didn't want our last note to get buried.

If you're up for leaving a quick review or sending a photo of the {{product_name}} in use, here's the link again:

[Google review link]

And if now's not a good time, no problem at all — we won't keep following up on this.

{{sender_name}}
ALINE Manufacturing
